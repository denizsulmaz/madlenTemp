Feature patterns · 1 / 4

what repeats across all eleven● Madlen has it● partial● gap

Feature patterns

  • Snap a question → worked solution. Table stakes now, not a differentiator. Madlen ships it Socratically, which is the better version. Astra, Knowunity, Pakodemy, DK Koçum
  • Upload your own source → grounded tutor. Madlen already takes uploaded documents and YouTube links alongside assigned work and the ready library. YouLearn built a whole company on this one capability; we ship it as an option inside a bigger product and say almost nothing about it. YouLearn, Studocu, Astra
  • Deneme in → weakness map → plan rebuilt. Madlen has the deneme analysis and the follow-up questions. The half that's missing is the last one: a plan that tells the student what to do tomorrow and reshapes itself as they work. That is precisely what DK Koçum charges ₺4,000–7,500/month for. DK Koçum, Pakodemy, Atom
  • Mode switching (Solve / Socratic / Practice). Madlen is Socratic by design and never hands over the answer. That's the right default for schools, but Astra proves students also want a straight solve. One product, two modes, chosen by the school. Astra, Thea
  • Adult in the loop. Madlen has the teacher, and puts them in the marking path. Every high-ARPU product here adds a second adult: a coach (DK), a parent (Seesaw), a tutor (Atom). Madlen has neither. DK Koçum, Seesaw, Atom
  • AI marks open-ended work, teacher confirms. Madlen already runs the posture Graide sells to universities: AI proposes, a human approves. That is a stronger compliance story than any fully-automated marker on this map. Atom, Graide, Seesaw

Business model · 2 / 4

how they make money● Madlen has it● partial● gap

Business model

  • Three price tiers, three orders of magnitude — and we bill in the wrong unit. Study apps $7–20/mo · outcome prep £40–70/mo · human coaching ₺4,000–7,500/mo per student. Madlen bills the school per student per year. DK Koçum earns more from one student in a fortnight than we do in a year. The gap is not product cost, it is who is held responsible for the result.
  • Every consumer product here has an open free tier; Madlen has none. Access today is a demo trial for teachers on request, which is a B2B motion with a human on the other end. In UK teacher-B2C there is nobody to request a demo from.
